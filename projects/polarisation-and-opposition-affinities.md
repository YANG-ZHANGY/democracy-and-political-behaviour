# Polarisation and opposition affinities

[繁體中文](polarisation-and-opposition-affinities.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

Does sharing a political opponent also imply positive feelings toward other opposition parties?

## The idea

This line of inquiry examines the overlap of favourable party evaluations across individuals. It separates a common adversary from shared political affinity and asks what the distribution of evaluations reveals about fragmented opposition constituencies. Favourable evaluations and willingness to cooperate are treated as distinct political judgments.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Democracy and Political Behaviour](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
