# Democratic support and unconstrained leadership

[繁體中文](democratic-support-and-leadership.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

What does support for democracy mean when it coexists with approval of a leader freed from institutional restraints?

## The idea

The project examines how evaluations of democracy and strong leadership combine within the same person. It distinguishes the size of a joint constituency from the relationship between its component attitudes, and considers response intensity, confidence in political institutions and different meanings of democracy.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Democracy and Political Behaviour](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
