# Anti-pluralism and civic space

[繁體中文](anti-pluralism-and-civic-space.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

When do parties that reject pluralism acquire the opportunity to restrict independent organisation?

## The idea

This research connects party commitments with governing relationships. It asks how occupying government, supporting a governing coalition or remaining in opposition changes the opportunities to constrain civic organisations. The central distinction is between restrictive preferences and the institutional means to act on them.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Democracy and Political Behaviour](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
