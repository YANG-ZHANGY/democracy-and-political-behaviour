# Democratic gatekeeping and political acceptance

[繁體中文](democratic-gatekeeping.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Concept note

## Research question

How do established parties define the boundary between legitimate competition and threats to democratic rules?

## The idea

The concept note considers how public recognition, coalition signals and institutional cooperation can change the political standing of populist challengers. It asks when gatekeeping is sustained and how electoral incentives complicate commitments to democratic standards.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Democracy and Political Behaviour](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
