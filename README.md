# Democracy and Political Behaviour

[繁體中文](README.zh-Hant.md) · [Yang ZHANG](https://github.com/YANG-ZHANGY)

Democratic commitments, institutional constraints and the political meaning of public support.

## Research projects

| Project | Central question |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Anti-pluralism and civic space](projects/anti-pluralism-and-civic-space.md) | When do parties that reject pluralism acquire the opportunity to restrict independent organisation? |
| [Democratic support and unconstrained leadership](projects/democratic-support-and-leadership.md) | What does support for democracy mean when it coexists with approval of a leader freed from institutional restraints? |
| [Polarisation and opposition affinities](projects/polarisation-and-opposition-affinities.md) | Does sharing a political opponent also imply positive feelings toward other opposition parties? |
| [Democratic gatekeeping and political acceptance](projects/democratic-gatekeeping.md) | How do established parties define the boundary between legitimate competition and threats to democratic rules? |

## Research orientation

The emphasis is on political questions, institutional mechanisms and the relationships between actors. Project pages introduce the ideas in accessible language.
